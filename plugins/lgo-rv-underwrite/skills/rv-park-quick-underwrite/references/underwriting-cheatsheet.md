# RV Park Underwriting Cheat Sheet (LGO)

*From first look to submitted offer. Buy on what it does TODAY, not what it could do.*

## The 8 Steps

1. **Gather docs** — P&L (2–3 yrs), T12, rent roll / reservation report, OM, tax returns.
2. **Park basics** — Site count & types, unit mix, infrastructure (city vs septic), occupancy mix.
3. **Market research** — Demand drivers, 5–8 competitors (rates, amenities, reviews), market cap rate.
4. **Run financials** — Top-line trend, conservative NOI, adjust expenses up, value it today.
5. **Find value-add** — Fill vacancy, raise to market rent, bill back utilities, then capex items.
6. **Build pro forma** — Year 1 flat revenue + higher expenses, model the ramp, 3% growth after.
7. **Choose financing** — Know seller's debt. DSCR, seller carry, Subject-to, Morby Method, or lease-option.
8. **Send the offer** — LOI. Protect both sides. If overpriced, lead with terms not a low price.

## The Numbers That Matter

- **NOI** = Income − Operating Expenses
- **Value today** = Adjusted NOI ÷ Market Cap Rate
- **Implied cap** = Adjusted NOI ÷ Asking Price. *Below 8% = overpriced.*
- **Quick NOI** = Revenue × 50% (conservative)
- **Cap rate trick**: suburban C-class apartment cap rate + 4 pts. RV parks usually 8–10% (~9%).

**Adjust expenses UP for a new owner:**

- **Property taxes** — reassessed on sale, almost always higher. The big one.
- **Labor / mgmt** — normalize to market (free-rent manager is understated).
- **Insurance** — expect a modest bump.
- **Reserves** — set cash aside; lenders require it.

## Financing Options & Full Cash to Close

- **DSCR loan** — based on NOI, ~70% LTV. Low NOI = low appraisal.
- **Seller carry** — seller finances part; bridges the gap.
- **Morby Method** — Senior DSCR loan + seller carry in 2nd lien position; appraisal still applies.
- **Lease + option** — lease now, buy later; time to raise NOI first.

**Cash to close is NOT just the down payment:**
Down payment + CapEx + Reserves (6 mo) + lender debt-service reserve.

**Goal:** path to $10K+/month cash flow within ~1 year of stabilizing.

## Golden Rules

1. Value what it does today, not what it could do.
2. NOI ÷ cap rate is the whole valuation game.
3. Always run conservative, realistic, upside. Offer on conservative.
4. When price won't work, terms can.
5. Write down every question. It is a conversation, not a one-shot.
